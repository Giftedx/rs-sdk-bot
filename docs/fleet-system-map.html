Goals and useful demand

DemandEngine values unresolved owner goal effects and finite consumer chains. Gold has no standalone value without a named supply or kit shortage.

GoalUseful resultEvidence boundary
Main OverallA fresh rank-one result under the record, after the final route passes its launch conditions.Model ticks, rehearsal ticks, launch permission and a recorded board rank are separate facts.
Specialist boardsFresh owned wins on skill boards with priced route margins (packages/brain/src/campaigns/specialists.ts).Declarative campaign specs track declared targets and gate launch on fresh snapshots, rehearsals and staged supplies.
Every questComplete implemented journal names observed across owned live accounts.Script coverage or a seeded practice completion does not supply a live journal observation.
Useful items and best gearRecent stock capped at named route and army-kit demand, with delivery where required.This finite subset does not define the full useful-item or best-in-slot collection.
PK armyDeclared accounts reach 99 Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged, Magic, Prayer and Hitpoints, then win against players and hold the Wilderness.Base combat levels, credited player kills and Wilderness control each need separate evidence.
  1. WorldSnapshot → DemandEngine

    Compute finite goal demand

    packages/brain/src/snapshot.ts reads live statuses, synced fleet snapshots, reserves, market quotes, spot occupancy and active transfers into a canonical WorldSnapshot.

    packages/brain/src/demand.ts emits finite GoalDemand items for main_supply, specialist_prep, quest_board, gear_collection, pk_army, squad_supply and coin_fund.

    packages/planner/src/supply-bill.ts and packages/brain/src/campaigns.ts derive exact item shortages for Haggis and ready specialist campaigns while protecting reserved stock.

  2. ActivityCatalog + RateStore

    Price candidate methods

    packages/brain/src/catalog.ts registers activities across all 9 unified worker families with inputs, outputs, spots, trust tiers and baseline rates.

    packages/brain/src/rate-store.ts blends observed live and practice RateSample throughput with baseline estimates so measured methods outrank slower alternatives.

  3. OutcomeClassifier

    Turn run endings into structured state

    packages/brain/src/outcome-classifier.ts classifies worker exits into normal completion, transient transport/infra faults, missing prerequisites, deaths and worker defects.

    Repeated defects quarantine the specific activity fault signature rather than blocking unrelated work; deaths hold the account until operator review.

How Fleet Brain plans

Deterministic planning scores 1-to-3-step account chains per end-to-end hour and schedules the whole side together.

  1. AccountPlanner

    Build 1-to-3-step dependency chains

    packages/brain/src/planner.ts evaluates each eligible account against active demands, prepending needed recover, acquire, gear_upgrade or unlock steps before target work.

    packages/brain/src/preflight.ts enforces skill, quest, trust-tier, protected-clock, reserve and cooldown constraints before an activity enters candidate scoring.

    Every chain carries an end-to-end scorePerHour including acquisition, travel and unlock ticks.

  2. FleetScheduler

    Allocate slots, spots and transfers

    packages/brain/src/scheduler.ts prioritizes recovery queue entries, applies the retention margin (15% default) to avoid thrashing running bots, respects maxBots and spot concurrency caps, and emits safe hub transfers.

    Accounts without useful work or blocked by holds receive explicit entries in idleReasons.

  3. brain-runtime.ts

    Drain commands and emit FleetState

    On each cycle, packages/fleet/src/brain-runtime.ts drains validated operator commands from .practice/live/operator-commands/, runs the planner/scheduler, and atomically writes .practice/live/fleet-state.json.

The account life cycle

An account needs a proved useful job, a registered identity and one owning side. Credentials alone do not make it ready to play.

  1. Naming and ownership

    Reserve one identity

    packages/core/src/names.ts defines ordinary account names (1–12 alphanumeric characters); packages/core/src/specialist-boards.ts reserves specialist names so ordinary accounts never take them.

    fleet/owners.json assigns each account to one fleet side (local, cloud, claude-cloud, claude-cloud-2). openBot refuses accounts owned by another side.

  2. Credentials and registration

    Create, claim and confirm logout

    scripts/fleet.ts manages local credentials and vault sealing (scripts/fleet-vault.ts, AES-256-GCM via packages/core/src/fleet-vault.ts). scripts/claim-account.ts registers a new live name and confirms logout.

    The first login starts the account's live clock. Protected main and specialist accounts stay offline until their launch gates pass.

  3. Account lifecycle states

    Keep blocked accounts explicit

    Brain tracks each account in paused_owner, reserved, dead, needs_logout_recovery, needs_location_recovery, running, in_trade or idle. Non-expiring states (paused_owner, dead) require explicit operator action.

A job becomes a bot

Swarm controls admission. The worker owns its session. The connection producer records what the server confirms.

goes onnext stepwaitsdepends on a clearing eventretriesbounded repeatholdsrepair or owner action needed
  1. Swarm + worker-dispatch.ts

    Check account and host admission

    • holdsReservations, wrong side, stop files, quarantined defects or open InfraCircuitBreaker hold launches.
    • waitsAdmission counts active, external and pending accounts against maxBots and keeps free RAM above max(4 GiB, minFreeGB).
    • holdsSession admission checks the account's producer logout proof in .practice/live/session-lifecycle/.
  2. Detached supervisor

    Start and monitor the worker

    packages/practice/src/supervise.ts (or supervise-host.ts for hosted runs) spawns the worker, enforces stop files and fleet leases, keeps bounded RESULT/LEDGER output, and classifies end reasons.

  3. openBot → connectInProcess

    Cross the network boundary

    packages/core/src/bot.ts and packages/core/src/inprocess.ts verify side ownership, launch gates, stop files, controller lease, worker release digest and shared account claim before connecting.

  4. Worker session

    Observe, act and settle

    packages/core/src/status.ts writes live status to .practice/live/status/<account>.json (and practice status to .practice/sim/status/) every 5 ticks and 5 s. Tick records go to .practice/ticks/.

    Settlement prints LEDGER, confirms logout with the server, writes logout proof and prints final RESULT.

Code reaches the fleet

GitHub Actions standard runners verify pull requests. live-update.ts fast-forwards master safely and triggers affected daemon reloads.

  1. GitHub Actions CI

    Run full suites and benches on hosted runners

    .github/workflows/ci.yml runs linux (2 shards, check-pin, check-no-diary, typecheck, isolated packages/dashboard tests), windows (6 shards via tools/ci-gate.ts --full), route-key-parity, hosted multi-seed bench-cases, pvp-practice, preparation-practice and native-practice.

    Local checks stay fast (< 3 min): bun test <changed-files>, bun tools/typecheck-narrow.ts and bun tools/check-no-diary.ts --list.

  2. Fleet agent → live-update

    Fast-forward master safely

    packages/fleet/src/live-update.ts advances master to origin/master when no live run loads a changed file and no active campaign runs, while preserving dirty local edits and honoring LIVE_UPDATE=0 or stop-live-update.

  3. live-update → daemon reload

    Reload affected controllers

    .practice/live-update.json marks daemons whose import closure overlaps changed files; packages/core/src/live-reload.ts triggers clean reloads between cycles without losing active work.

Supply acquisition

packages/brain/src/acquire.ts chooses the lowest-cost feasible source for each missing item in canonical priority order.

  1. AcquireItem ladder

    Resolve missing inputs

    Sources are evaluated in order: own_bank → npc_shop → ge → hub_banker → craft_gather, preserving DEFAULT_OPERATING_COIN_FLOAT and account/global reserves.

    Shop catalogs filter to runnable derived shop profiles; GE pricing reads .practice/market-all.json; hub transfers require idle verified donor and recipient logout proofs plus anti-ping-pong checks.

Orders and trades

Cross-side item movement uses explicit fleet orders and bilateral settlement receipts on the fleet-data branch.

  1. fleet-order → fleet-sync → fleet-trade

    Move items between sides safely

    scripts/fleet-order.ts (or scripts/route-stage.ts) writes an order under fleet/orders/. fleet-sync.ts shares orders, receipts and snapshots across sides.

    Each side's fleet-agent.ts runs scripts/fleet-trade.ts when its participant is idle with confirmed logout. packages/core/src/fleet-trade-settlement.ts reconciles bilateral receipts into settled, failed or reconcile.

Sides and ownership

Each machine runs bots only for its own FLEET_SIDE. Main and specialist accounts never transfer sides.

fleet/owners.json on fleet-data records account ownership. packages/core/src/fleet-lease.ts and packages/core/src/fleet-trade-claim.ts coordinate authoritative Git ref claims so two machines cannot log in the same account concurrently. Sealed credentials live in fleet/vault/ and restore via FLEET_VAULT_KEY.

Watching and control

The dashboard joins live observations with Brain state. Operator commands flow through validated files under .practice/live/operator-commands/.

  1. Local dashboard

    Show live state and Brain chains

    Default address is 127.0.0.1:3100. packages/dashboard/server.ts joins live status files, canonical fleet snapshots, world feed, hiscores, host metrics and .practice/live/fleet-state.json every 2 s over SSE.

    Responses mask the main account name. /api/control enforces loopback origin, session nonce and DASH_READ_ONLY=1.

  2. Publisher → hosted views

    Publish private control and public spectator projections

    packages/dashboard/publish.ts pushes owner-only control.json to the data branch and whitelisted public.json (excluding main/practice accounts, banks and private task text) to GitHub Pages site.

  3. OperatorCommand flow

    Apply validated commands once

    packages/dashboard/operator-control.ts and packages/dashboard/publish-commands.ts write validated OperatorCommand JSON files to .practice/live/operator-commands/, which brain-runtime.ts drains into applied/ or rejected/.

Limits and proof boundaries

The codebase keeps unproved claims explicit across planners, proofs and dashboards.

  • Tests, practice benches and isolated rehearsals do not prove live procurement, market depth or recorded hiscore wins.
  • Main and specialist launch gates protect scored clocks; tool access or practice passes do not authorize a scored login.
  • The owner goal scoreboard leaves full specialist/collection scope, credited Wilderness kills and Wilderness control explicit until backed by live proof.
  • Incoming dependency manifest changes require manual checkout and lockfile updates rather than automatic live-update fast-forwards.
  • Host slot expansion requires measured RAM, CPU and game-tick headroom alongside real finite demand.
