# Audit B, 2026-10-22 (after the Phase 2 merge)

Scope: goal, strategy, architecture, evidence. Each finding has its evidence.

## Critical

C1. No end-to-end specialist exists. Every proven script starts from a practice preset that injects levels,
food, and position (`packages/practice/src/presets.ts`, e.g. `thieve_knights`: Thieving 55, HP 50, 20 lobsters,
already in Ardougne). The live server has no presets. The first real fresh-account test, the trip to Ardougne,
died 4 times in 2,198 ticks (`research/thieving-ladder.md`). Fix: the next proof target is one specialist from a
fresh account to a board level, on the practice server with no preset.

C2. One bot process uses about 460 MB for pathfinding alone (measured: RSS 49 MB -> 460 MB after
`initPathfinding()`, 14.4 s). The design plans up to 30 accounts online (spec section 9): about 14 GB before
the client. This machine had 5.8 to 9.2 GB free, and Claude Code killed long jobs 5 times today for low memory.
Fix: run many sessions in one process with one shared pathfinding copy (the in-process shim allows it).

## High

H1. The board margins leave out travel, food, deaths, and helper logistics. `research/board-ranking.md` counts
tutorial + method rate + level-up dialogs only. Measured gaps: the Ardougne trip (not counted; the land route
kills), and the Runecraft helper refill trip (about 150 ticks, not measured). The +823 (Runecraft) and +600
(Thieving) margins are upper bounds, not forecasts.

H2. The router knows walls and doors but not danger. It sent a 10 HP account past aggressive NPCs to death.
Fix: an NPC danger cost by combat level and aggression in `packages/core/src/world.ts`, or a safe-route list.

H3. Pathfinding loads lazily on the first walk, which can be after login (`vendor/rs-sdk/sdk/pathfinding.ts:206`).
Its 14 s sync load blocks the event loop while the account is in the world and its playtime counts: about
26 ticks at the live 539 ms. Small for Thieving, fatal for Cooking (leader 38 ticks). Fix: call
`initPathfinding()` before `connectInProcess`. Not yet measured on a live session.

H4. Rates rest on few samples. Thieving model fit uses one data point (guard), and its man rate is 2-3x the
measured one. Most rates have 2-3 runs. Decisions made on these numbers can flip.

## Medium

M1. Only Claude plays. GPT and Gemini are not wired (Gemini CLI broken). The owner asked for all three.
M2. The live tick is not measured without login (539 ms with login, one session).
M3. Several boards cannot be won under the no-bug rule: Cooking (leader 38 ticks), and supply boards with
zero sellers (Crafting, Herblore, Smithing, Prayer). "Rank 1 on every board" needs a written list of
winnable boards, ranked by honest margin.
M4. The demo server belongs to another person. A wipe, a rule change, or a rate change resets the goal.

## What is solid
- Proof harness: fresh accounts, START check, unique names, infra-failure detection.
- Core actions: checked effects, cancellable walk legs, logout retries; 146 tests, 0 fail on master.
- Knowledge miner: re-derived spot-check of every field.
- Model-written scripts: 5 of 5 proven 3/3 (copper5, rcair, thieve300, thieveguards, thieveknights).
- Live login, tutorial skip, and logout work on the demo server (one smoke run).

## Recommendation
Fix C2 and H3 first (both are small and unblock everything). Then build one fresh-account specialist (C1)
with a safe route (H2), and re-rank boards with travel counted (H1).
