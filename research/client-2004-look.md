# The 2004 client look

Where the dashboard's game interface differs from the 2004 client, and the fix for each. Ground truth: the pinned client and content in `vendor/rs-sdk/server` (`webclient/src/client/Client.ts`, `content/sprites`, `content/scripts/**/interfaces/*.if`). Rule from the owner: the interface gets the 2004 look, and every RuneLite extra stays (HP orb, XP button, infoboxes); a fix restyles or moves an extra, it does not remove the fact.

Each row: claim; evidence path; fix; gap.

| Part | 2004 client (evidence) | Dashboard | Fix | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimap extras | The minimap is `mapback`, the map, the compass and dots (`Client.ts:13934-13999`). No orbs. | HP and Prayer orbs, XP button, world-map globe on the minimap. | Keep them (owner's rule). Draw them as RuneLite draws its orbs, outside the 2004 frame art. | Art for a RuneLite-style orb on the 2004 frame is not chosen. |
| Frame art | One stone frame: `back.png` cut by `BACK_PIECES` (`engine/tools/pack/sprite/media.ts:14`), `invback.png` 190x261 panel body, `redstone1-3.png` on the selected tab, `mapback.png` 172x156 with the map at 25,5 and a 33 px compass. | CSS gradients, `interface-bg.png`, a drawn stone ring. | Export the `back.png` slices and `mapback.png` with magenta as alpha; build the side block at 249 px with `invback.png` and `redstone*.png` (copies are in `client-assets`, unused). | Large. The panel and chat are taller than the 2004 sizes; phone layouts need a retest. |
| Stats tab | Diagonal cell: current level top left, base level bottom right, p11 yellow; a bordered box with `QP`, `Combat Lvl`, `Total Lvl`; hover writes `XP` and `Next Level At` into that box (`content/scripts/player/interfaces/stats.if`). | `cur/base` on a gradient button; two lines; a pale floating tooltip. | Diagonal numbers in `RuneScape-Plain-11.ttf` (in `client-assets`, no `@font-face`); one bordered box; hover text in the box; keep Remaining XP as a third line. | The native pixel size of the Plain-11 face is not checked. |
| Loading and lost screens | Plain p12 white text with a 1 px shadow, no box: `Loading - please wait.`, `Connection lost`, `Please wait - attempting to reestablish` (`Client.ts:9434`, `4988-4991`). | A brown box, an orange `Welcome to RuneScape` heading, full stops. | No heading, no box, no full stops on the lost lines. | White text on a bright scene is harder to read. |
| Quest list | Names red `0xFF0000`, yellow, green `0x00FF00`, white on hover; `FREE QUESTS:` and `MEMBERS QUESTS:` headers (`questlist.if`). | Red `#ff3030`; no hover colour; no headers. | `#ff0000`; white on hover; keep the filter and the search field on one row. | Whether the snapshot carries a free or members flag is not checked. |
| Scrollbars | 16 px bar, arrows from `scrollbar.png`, track `0x23201b`, grip `0x4d4233` (`Client.ts:12966`). | Thin native bars. | `::-webkit-scrollbar` rules with those colours and `client-assets/scrollbar.png`; `scrollbar-color` as the Firefox fallback. | A 16 px bar takes room from a 190 px panel. |

Already as in 2004: the skill order and set, the chat input line, the private chat colour, the minimap dot colours, `Click here to logout`, the yellow stack counts, the side tab icons and their order with logout as a tab, the panel text colours and titles, the worn equipment slots and their empty pictures, the right-click menu, and the chat buttons `Public chat` and `Private chat` with On in green and Off in red. The third chat button is `Game` (fleet events), which has no 2004 name. The chat buttons get the stone art with the frame art row.
