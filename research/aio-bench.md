# The AIO bench: findings

Command and rules: [script contract](../docs/script-contract.md#the-aio-bench). Engine patch: `patches/rs-sdk.md` item 10. Raw results: `.practice/aio-bench/`.

## Lockstep engine

**Claim:** a lockstep engine with one seed and a new engine for each case gives the same tick count on each run. **Conditions:** `bun run bench --smoke --no-keep`, seed 7, six engines at a time, three runs. **Evidence:** 15 of 15 cases equal in the three runs (`.practice/aio-bench/smkw1.log` to `smkw3.log`); the per-tick world traces of `crest` and `dwarfcannon` are byte-identical between two runs (`trA/`, `trB/`). **Gap:** a case with a partner account is not in the three-run check.

**Claim:** a shared engine makes one case change the result of the next. **Conditions:** seed 7, same engine build. **Evidence:** `dwarfcannon` 823 ticks after 20 earlier cases (`lockfull1.json`), 762 ticks on a new engine (`fresh1.json` to `fresh3.json`).

**Claim:** the lockstep tick counts agree with the 150 ms engine. **Conditions:** quests, one account, seed 7 against one 150 ms run. **Evidence:** `walk-to` 84 against 85; `barcrawl` 1401 against 1398; `biohazard` 1293 against 1292; `blackknight` 287 against 291 (`quests-base-s7.json`, `pass1.json`). On a wall-clock engine at 400 ms a tick, the live tick length, `biohazard` takes 1315 ticks against 1293 on lockstep, `blackknight` 292 against 287 and `imp` 9 against 9 (`.practice/aio-bench/bio400.json`, one run, `BENCH_TICK_MS=400 bun run bench <pattern> --wall-clock`). **Gap:** three quests and one run; combat quests differ by the random rolls.

**Claim:** 64 quests run in under 10 minutes. **Conditions:** six engines, this PC, no other load. **Evidence:** 588 s and 571 s wall (`quests-base-s7.log`, `quests-burst-s7.log`). The same quests took about 70 minutes on one 150 ms engine with four accounts in one world.

**Claim:** a shorter client loop is not usable. **Conditions:** `LOOP_MS` 1 and 5 against 20 in `vendor/rs-sdk/server/webclient/src/lite/session.ts`. **Evidence:** `biohazard` 1626 and 1595 ticks against 1294; wall time 25% lower. The client counts one frame for each loop, and animations end by frame count.

**Claim:** when a case gives two tick counts with one seed, the engine is not the cause. **Conditions:** `troll`, seed 7, two runs, each on a new engine with the per-tick world trace. **Evidence:** the two traces are byte-identical over 852 ticks (player and npc tile hashes, random draws for each tick), and the script reports 703 ticks in one run and 551 in the other (`.practice/aio-bench/trollA/`, `trollB/`). From about tick 400 the player tile does not change and the draw count is the same in both: the fight step makes no progress in either run. **Gap:** the part of the fight step that ends on the wall clock is not found; `bot.attack` in the SDK has limits in milliseconds.

## Routes

**Claim:** every catalog route is in one of two groups: 186 of 219 have a case that passes at seed 7, and 33 cannot run as one case and name the cause. **Conditions:** lockstep, a new engine for each case; the start state of each case comes from the worker's entry checks, a proof or the map files. **Evidence:** `bun run bench --coverage`; `.practice/aio-bench/baseline-seed7.json`; the 105 non-quest cases run in 271 s on six engines. **Gap:** a case proves one small job from one start state, not the route at every level or place.

**Claim:** `viking`, `squire` and `zanaris` pass at every seed tried. **Conditions:** seeds 7 to 10, a new engine for each case. **Evidence:** `viking` passes at 7, 8 and 9 (3001, 2727, 2787 ticks): the retreat rule fired in Koschei's fight, which ends at 0 hp by design, and `planLowHp` in `quest-lib` now switches the rule off for that fight. `squire` passes at four seeds with a wider bound on the cupboard search, and `zanaris` with a wider bound on the axe hunt.

**Claim:** `fluffs` fails when the kitten is in the west crate. **Conditions:** seed 9. **Evidence:** the crate at 3298,3514 is the one of six the bot does not reach: `bot.walkTo` answers "Stuck at (3307, 3506)", and `walkPath` clicks 120 times at that tile with no move (`.practice/aio-bench/output/quester_quest_fluffs.log`). **Gap:** the way round to that crate is not found.

**Claim:** `waterfall` stops at the Tree Gnome Village maze. **Evidence:** the walk to the maze mouth 2505,3190 ends at 2509,3187 at any tolerance; the planner has no route from there to the first maze waypoint 2512,3190; the route search to the goal goes round the hedge to 2529,3140, where the client refuses the walk. **Gap:** the tile where the maze opens is not found.

**Claim:** five quests fail at one or two of four seeds on a random roll they do not recover from. **Conditions:** seeds 7 to 10, a new engine for each case. **Evidence:** `eadgar` fails at 7 and 9 (retreat with no food, death); `mm` at 7 and 9 (`talk garkor`); `legends` at 9 and 10; `totem` at 10; `troll` at 7 and 10 in one run and passes at 7 in another (`.practice/aio-bench/quests-seeds-7-10.log`). **Gap:** no fix exists for any of them.

**Claim:** `Q_BURST=1` breaks 20 quests that pass without it and saves 0.7% on the rest. **Conditions:** seed 7, 64 quests. **Evidence:** 44 of 67 pass with the flag against 61 of 64 without; 30,203 ticks against 30,426 on the 41 quests that pass both ways; the errors are `dialogue click refused` and a failed talk step (`quests-burst-s7.json`). Three of the failures repeat on the 150 ms engine. Largest gains: `chompybird` 772 to 474, `murder` 261 to 204.

## Recorded dialogue plans

**Claim:** a recorded dialogue plan makes ten quests faster: 12,632 ticks against 13,912 over nine of them at three seeds (9.2% fewer). **Conditions:** seeds 7, 8 and 9, a new engine for each case; `scripts/generated/talk-plans.json` holds the clicks each talk took in a passing run, and `talk` sends them in one write. **Evidence:** `cook` 8 against 18; `imp` 9 against 22; `hetty` 53, 48, 48 against 68, 66, 62; `hauntedmine` 791, 853, 823 against 929, 1052, 1181; `cog`, `arena`, `grail`, `ikov` and `murder` fewer in total (`.practice/aio-bench/seed-7.json` to `seed-9.json` against `quests-seeds-7-10.log`). `fluffs` fails at one seed of three with and without a plan. **Gap:** a plan breaks a talk whose step check reads page text: the plan answers pages before the client shows them, and 35 of 50 quests failed with their recorded plans (`quests-plans.log`). Reading the text from the packets repairs most of them and removes the gain. The sweep mode names its quest script in `QUEST_SCRIPT_ID`, so it uses the same plans: Cook's Assistant through the sweep takes 8 ticks against 18.

## Burst banking

**Claim:** one "deposit all" for each item id in the same tick does not make a bench case faster. **Conditions:** seed 7, `depositPack` in `packages/core/src/deposit.ts` with the burst against one item id a tick; the three cases that call it. **Evidence:** `market:shop-buy` 575 ticks with the burst against 420 without; `skiller:fletching:arrows` and `skiller:thieving:knights` end at their tick cap in both, and the knights case makes no bank trip in 1,500 ticks (`.practice/aio-bench/burst1.log`, `burst0.log`). The change is not in the code. **Gap:** no bench case banks a pack of many item ids, so the bench cannot show the gain the rival states (24 to 5 ticks for 24 item ids); `market:shop-buy` gave 402 and 420 ticks in two runs of the same code.

## Rival comparison

**Claim:** rs-stun-toolkit states fewer ticks than ours on 11 quests; the causes are a planned dialogue burst and an amulet of glory in the kit. **Conditions:** their lockstep sim with their kit and stats against our `quest_<slug>` presets at seed 7; code read on both sides, nothing of theirs run here. **Evidence:** their `run-<quest>.share.md` medians in `RIVAL_QUEST_TICKS` (`scripts/measure/aio-bench-cases.ts`); estimated savings: `mortton` 400 to 550, `dragon` 220 to 270, `grail` 200 to 280, `squire` 150, `scorpion` 130, `merlin` 110 to 130, `fishingcompo` 100, `hunt` 60 to 80, `gobdip` 25 to 30, `cook` and `sheep` 10 each. **Gap:** every saving is an estimate until its port runs on the bench; their `mortton` run carries the materials that ours buys.

**Claim:** their bot layer sends more per tick than ours in three places. **Evidence:** a whole conversation with its choice clicks in one write (`speedrun/legs/q-lib.ts` `talk`) against one page a tick (`scripts/generated/quest-lib.ts` `converse`); deposits and withdrawals in one burst (`speedrun/core.ts` `bankSwap`) against one deposit for each item id (`packages/core/src/deposit.ts` `depositPack`); up to five inventory actions a tick (`core.ts` `burst`) against one. **Gap:** none is measured here.

## The engine drops a skill action when an AFK event is ready

**Claim:** the pinned engine sets `afkEventReady` for a player on a roll every 500 ticks. The next cooking, mining or other skill script that tests `afk_event` then returns with no message and no effect. With `NODE_RANDOM_EVENTS` off, no event spawns, so the action is lost for nothing. The local patch makes `afk_event` false when `NODE_RANDOM_EVENTS` is off. **Conditions:** lockstep, seed 7, `quester:mode:tbwt-owned`. **Evidence:** `.practice/aio-bench/trace/quester_mode_tbwt-owned.actions` shows `OpLocU` at tick 806 and `ran=true` at tick 807, and the bot gets no cook message; `vendor/rs-sdk/server/content/scripts/skill_cooking/scripts/cooking.rs2` returns after `afk_event`; with the patch the case passes in 1346 ticks. **Gap:** a server without this patch still drops the action, so a worker must send a skill action again when it has no effect; 35 content scripts test `afk_event`.

## The action trace

**Claim:** `BENCH_TRACE=<dir> bun run bench <pattern>` writes `<case>.actions` beside `<case>.trace`. A line with `in` is one message from a bot and the result of its handler. A line with `act` is one tick in which the player holds a target: `access` is false when the player is delayed, protected or has a window open, `ran` is true when the engine started the script for the action. **Conditions:** lockstep engine, `SIM_ACTION_FILE` set by the practice stack. **Evidence:** `vendor/rs-sdk/server/engine/src/simseed.ts`, `NetworkPlayer.ts`, `Player.ts`. A line with `hunt` is one NPC that could hunt the player: `candidate`, or `skip=` with the check that refused (`too-strong`, `player-in-combat`, `npc-in-combat`, `var`, `inv`, `player-busy`, `player-afk`). **Gap:** the trace has no line for a script that an NPC runs.

## A shade attack locks out other shades for 100 ticks

**Claim:** a shadow that attacks sets bit 4 of `%morttonmulti` on the player, and the hunt rule of every shadow skips a player with that bit set until `shadeattack_reset` clears it 100 ticks later. A shadow by the entrance door of the Fiyr lair can set it while the bot walks in, so no shadow in the hunt room attacks for up to 100 ticks. The worker waits `SHADE_INCOMING_TICKS` (125) for its first shade. **Conditions:** lockstep, seed 7, `fighter:shade`. **Evidence:** `.practice/aio-bench/trace/fighter_shade.actions` has `hunt npc=shadeshadow_level5 ... candidate` at tick 277 at the door and `skip=var 340&16:536870928` on each later tick in the hunt room; `vendor/rs-sdk/server/content/scripts/minigames/game_mortton/scripts/mortton_shades.rs2`; the case passes in 744 ticks. **Gap:** one seed.

## The knife reset on a fresh woodcutter

**Claim:** from a fresh account to Woodcutting 45, the knife reset takes 6.9% fewer ticks than plain chopping: 984, 880 and 716 ticks against 1064, 948 and 758 at seeds 7, 8 and 9 (2580 against 2770). **Conditions:** lockstep, `skiller:woodcutting:fresh45`, preset `wc_fresh`, XP rate 25; plain chopping is the same case with `--set WC_KNIFE=0`. **Evidence:** `bun run bench '^skiller:woodcutting:fresh45$' --all --seeds 7,8,9`, then the same with `--set WC_KNIFE=0`. **Gap:** the run includes the walk, the pickpocket for coins and the axe purchase, so the saving on the chop alone is larger than 6.9%.

## The knife reset on the Ardougne zoo iron

**Claim:** on the six iron rocks south of the Ardougne zoo, from Mining 64 with a rune pickaxe, the rock rotation with the knife reset mines an ore each 3.22, 3.19 and 3.19 ticks (272, 274 and 274 XP a tick) at seeds 7, 8 and 9. The rotation with no knife takes 4.32 ticks at each seed (202 XP a tick), and the drop worker with no rotation 702 ticks for the 75 ores at each seed (93 XP a tick). The rival figure is 3.00 ticks at respawn 13. **Conditions:** lockstep, one account on the engine, so the iron respawn is 18 ticks from the depletion seen to the rock seen again (`skill_mining/configs/mine.dbrow:72` 20, scaled in `general/scripts/player_count.rs2:4`); 75 ores, Mining 64 to 72; XP rate 25. Ticks per ore count from the first ore to the last (`MINING_RATE`). **Evidence:** `bun run bench '^extra:mining-zoo-' --seed <n>` for seeds 7, 8 and 9; the `RESULT` and `MINING_RATE` lines in `.practice/aio-bench/output/extra_mining-zoo-*.log`. Each tile beside a zoo rock touches one rock, so each ore needs a step, and the swing starts the tick after the step; the cut in the click tick sets the roll 2 ticks later all the same. With all six rocks down, the cut 2 ticks before a rock is seen again makes the click roll in its own tick (`mining.rs2:83-84`), so the six rocks give six ores in 19 ticks. **Gap:** at respawn 18 the six-rock limit is 19 / 6 = 3.17 ticks; the rival 3.00 needs a seen respawn of 17 or less. The seen 18 is one less than the scaled 19, so 17 needs a scaled 18, which is more than 200 players online (ESTIMATE from that offset). A gem roll (1 in 256, `mining.rs2:194`) costs one 3-tick roll.

In 600 ticks the account gains the same Thieving XP with and without the bones at each seed (432037, 446790 and 307695 at seeds 7, 8 and 9), and the 22 dragon bones in the pack add 39600 Prayer XP at each seed. ## A script that acts only in tick callbacks

**Claim:** the lockstep client sends its tick acknowledgement at once only when the script waits for a tick through `watchTickStream`, `waitTicks` or a state wait. A script that acts only inside `onTick` callbacks is not seen as waiting, so each tick waits for `SIM_BUSY_MS` (2000 ms). Such a script raises `tickWaits.active` for its callback loop. **Conditions:** `SIM_LOCKSTEP=1`. **Evidence:** `scripts/measure/stun-guest.ts` took more than 500 s for 600 ticks and takes about 60 s with the counter raised; `packages/core/src/inprocess.ts`. **Gap:** no check finds other scripts of this kind.

## The Flamtaer temple repair

**Claim:** one Repair click repeats by itself every 3 ticks (`p_oploc(3)`), and a wall goes up one level for each attempt that succeeds once the build total is past the step for its level. A bot that stays on one wall until level 10 and buys its building sets in one trip rebuilds the temple faster than a bot that clicks the lowest wall after each level: `mortton` takes 1124, 1041 and 1118 ticks at seeds 7, 8 and 9, against 1506 at seed 7 with the lowest-wall order and three trips of two sets. Swamp paste stacks, so a set takes two pack slots. **Conditions:** lockstep, `quester:quest:mortton`, Crafting 99. **Evidence:** `flamtaer_temple.rs2:18-158`; `scripts/generated/quest-mortton.ts`; `bun run bench '^quester:quest:mortton$' --all --seeds 7,8,9`. **Gap:** the pack holds seven sets and the temple needs about eight, so one late trip remains (about 60 ticks); the rival states 944 ticks; no shade attacked the walls in these runs.

## Refused actions and the quiet gap after them

**Claim:** an action that a script sends while the engine holds the player (a delay of one tick after a placement, a knockback, an open window) is refused with no message, and the script then waits its whole bound before it sends again. `bun scripts/measure/trace-waste.ts <trace dir>` ranks the cases of a trace folder by these quiet gaps. Over the 69 quest cases at seed 7 it lists 25 cases that lose 20 ticks or more, 2543 ticks in all. Two are repaired: `waterfall` sent each pillar rune one tick early and waited 15 ticks (1364 to 1112 ticks), and `troll` used `bot.attack`, which waited about 110 ticks after a refused click while the bot stood idle (it now passes at seeds 7, 8 and 9 in 753, 761 and 767 ticks). **Conditions:** lockstep, `BENCH_TRACE=<dir> bun run bench '^quester:quest:' --all --no-keep`. **Evidence:** `scripts/measure/trace-waste.ts`; `.practice/aio-bench/trace/quester_quest_troll.actions` has no attack tick between tick 194 and tick 302. **Gap:** a quiet gap inside a fight that goes on by itself is not a loss, so the list is a place to look, not a measured saving; 18 other calls of `bot.attack` in the quest scripts are not checked.

## A direct attack click against `bot.attack`

**Claim:** `q.attack` (one attack click with no wait, `scripts/generated/quest-lib.ts`) in place of `bot.attack` is not a gain in every fight. Of the 13 quests that used `bot.attack`, three are faster or equal with it at seeds 7, 8 and 9 and keep it: `zombiequeen` 2339, 1710 and 2369 ticks (2950 at seed 7 with `bot.attack`), `routequest` 670, 664 and 678 (687), `biohazard` 1284, 1304 and 1286 (1293). Two got worse and keep `bot.attack`: `ikov` failed at all three seeds, and `regicide` took 3452 ticks at seed 7 against 3239. The other eight changed by less than 2% and keep `bot.attack`. **Conditions:** lockstep, `bun run bench '<the 13 quests>' --all --seeds 7,8,9`. **Evidence:** `.practice/aio-bench/attack-seeds.log`. **Gap:** the cause of the `ikov` failure and the `regicide` loss with the direct click is not found; a fight that needs the walk and the wait of `bot.attack` is the likely cause.

## A step sends again soon after a refusal

**Claim:** `step` in `scripts/generated/quest-lib.ts` reads `opFeedback.opRejectedCount`. When the count rises after a send and no effect comes in 2 ticks, it sends again, at most 4 more times. Without this the step waited its whole bound after each action that the engine refused. Over the 29 quest cases that the change touches, seed 7 takes 39364 ticks against 41043 (4.09% fewer); the largest are `sheepherder` 1403 to 974, `legends` 4330 to 3974, `mm` 4620 to 4277 and `viking` 3001 to 2733. 27 of the 28 changed quests pass at seeds 8 and 9. `ikov` (878 to 1172 ticks) and `regicide` (fails at seeds 8 and 9) are worse with it and open the quest with `resend: false`, which keeps one send for each try. **Conditions:** lockstep; seed 7 for all 69 quests, seeds 8 and 9 for the 28 that changed, two practice servers at a time beside the live fleet. **Evidence:** `.practice/aio-bench/step-all.log`, `.practice/aio-bench/step-seeds.log`. **Gap:** an accepted action that stops the walk raises the count too, so a second send can reach an action that was accepted; the cause of the `ikov` and `regicide` losses is not found; the 41 quests with no change at seed 7 did not run at seeds 8 and 9 with the change.

## A skill case must run long enough to be a rate

**Claim:** a skill case that does one unit of work gives an XP figure for each 1,000 ticks that is not a rate: `cooking:wine` shows 82500000 from a run of 20 ticks. A case of about 1,000 ticks gives a rate that a change to the route can be judged against. Five default routes run that long with a target that the worker reaches before its cap: `woodcutting:yews` 1023 ticks, 37 logs, 158236 XP for each 1,000 ticks; `crafting:inventory` 941 ticks, 180 gems, 657545; `herblore:potions` 598 ticks, 92 potions, 336538; `prayer:bury` 128 ticks, 55 bones, 48336. **Conditions:** lockstep, seed 7, XP rate 25, the level and stock of each case's start state. **Evidence:** `scripts/measure/aio-bench-cases.ts`; the `RESULT` line of each run. `mining:ore` runs three full trips: 669 ticks, 81 ores. **Gap:** `firemaking:order`, `magic:order` and `agility:order` are short; a worker reports a fail when its cap ends before its target, so a rate case needs a target inside the cap; one seed.

## A level-up window cost the miner 60 ticks each time

**Claim:** a level-up window holds a mine action in the engine. The action trace shows `access=false modal=2` for the action, and no mining tick until the window closes. `mineRock` in `scripts/gather-ore.ts` looked for a window before its click, but the window can open in the tick of the click. It then waited its whole 60-tick timeout. The trace of a 1,200-tick run shows 14 gaps of 51 to 61 ticks after a click. `mineRock` now ends its wait when a window opens, closes it and clicks the rock again. ## The eat guard and the items a script needs

**Claim:** no fixed food list is free of quest items. 107 items heal. Quest scripts take 23 of them from the inventory (`inv_del` or `inv_total`), and they name each of the 14 cooked fish. Two quests take cooked fish: Family Crest (`quest_crest/scripts/crest_caleb.rs2`: swordfish, bass, tuna, salmon, shrimp) and Death Plateau (`quest_death/scripts/death_sherpa.rs2`: 10 trout). The eat guard (`packages/core/src/eat-guard.ts`) therefore eats only the 14 cooked fish, only after damage, only in a session whose script has sent an attack, and not in a session of a quest script or a microbot script. With those rules, a hill giant fight with cake, cheese, bread, redberry pie and stew in the first 5 slots and 20 lobsters ends with `kills=10 eaten=13 otherKept=5/5 hpMin=11`, and the same fight with `EAT_GUARD=0` ends with `RESULT fail died` at tick 82. The Cook's Assistant case prints no `EAT_GUARD` line. **Conditions:** lockstep, seed 1, one run each, `extra:giant-eat-guard` and `quester:quest:cook`, Hitpoints 20 and no armour in the fight. **Evidence:** `packages/core/test/eat-guard-content.test.ts` reads the pinned content and fails when a quest script outside its list takes a plain food item; `packages/core/test/eat-guard.test.ts` has the rule tests; the two cases print the lines above. **Gap:** no live run. One run for each condition is not a speed comparison. A script that fights, carries cooked fish for a purpose other than food and has no eater of its own is not covered: none is known.

## A preset food entry with a count is one slot

**Claim:** an inventory entry `{id: 379, count: 20}` in a preset is one slot that holds 20 lobsters. The server eats with `inv_delslot` (`consume_effects.rs2`), so one eat deletes all 20. A trace of the hill giant fight shows `hp=11 food=20` and then `hp=22 food=0`, and the bot dies. Food that a bot eats needs one entry for each piece. A script that uses `inv_del(inv, obj, 1)`, as cooking does, takes one piece from such a slot, so a preset with 28 raw fish in one entry is not affected. **Conditions:** lockstep, seed 1, `extra:giant-eat-guard`, `EAT_TRACE=1`. **Evidence:** the `EAT_TRACE` lines of the case with one entry of count 20. **Gap:** quest presets keep their entries with a count: a quest that needs more than 28 slots when each piece has its own slot was not checked.
