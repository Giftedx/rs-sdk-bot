# Quest routes to 99

Historical route sketch. Use [quest-plan](quest-plan.md) for source checks and measured segments. Leader times, XP totals and level conversions below require revalidation before planning. Guide requirements are hypotheses, not admission facts.

XP: `research/quest-xp.md` (this server's quest scripts, x25). Requirements: vendor/quest-helper guides
(OSRS rules; 2004 may differ, e.g. Dragon Slayer needed 32 quest points). Step guides: vendor/quest-helper
`helpers/quests/<quest>/*.java`; truth: `vendor/rs-sdk/server/content/scripts/quests/<quest>/`.
